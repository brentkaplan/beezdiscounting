# Example 21-item MCQ data

A dataset containing two illustrative participants' 21-item MCQ (Kirby &
Marakovic, 1996) responses: subject 1 responds like the median subject
in Kirby & Marakovic (1996) (switching between the eighth and ninth
ladder items, overall k of about 0.007) and subject 2 always chooses the
larger delayed reward. These responses are constructed for illustration
only, not drawn from an empirical sample.

## Usage

``` r
mcq21
```

## Format

Long-form data.frame with columns: subjectid, questionid, response.

## Source

Constructed in `data-raw/mcq21_data.R`.
